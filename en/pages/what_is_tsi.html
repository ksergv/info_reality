What is QI?

A Hypothesis about the Physical Nature of QI

In this concept, QI is the physical manifestation of coherence at the informational level of reality.

The information field is considered an independent level connected to the quantum field. Its state can be represented as a complex quantity:

\[ \Phi_I = A_I e^{i\phi_I} \]

where \(A_I\) is the amplitude of the informational state, and \(\phi_I\) is its phase.

A change in the informational state is accompanied by a change in phase:

\[ \phi_I \rightarrow \phi_I + \delta\phi_I \]

If the information field is quantized, its disturbances may exist in the form of quanta carrying the phase of that disturbance. In this sense, an informational qubit can be regarded as the minimal coherent state capable of carrying informational phase.

Under coherent coupling between the informational and quantum levels, a connection arises:

\[ \Phi_I \leftrightarrow \Psi_Q \]

A phase disturbance of the information field can then change the phase of the quantum state:

\[ \phi_Q' = \phi_Q + \delta\phi_I \]

Here \(\phi_Q\) is the initial phase of the quantum state, \(\delta\phi_I\) is the phase disturbance at the informational level, and \(\phi_Q'\) is the resulting phase of the quantum state.

Thus: In this model, QI is neither a separate material substance nor an electromagnetic field. It is a coherent excitation of the information field capable, through phase coupling, of manifesting itself in quantum and subsequently physical reality.

Schematically, the process can be represented as follows:

\[ \text{information field} \rightarrow \text{phase disturbance} \rightarrow \text{informational quantum}] →coherent coupling→quantum field→physical manifestation

In this interpretation, QI is the carrier and manifestation of a coherent phase relationship between the informational and physical levels of reality.

The presented justification is a hypothesis and does not constitute an established principle of modern physics, but it may be useful for understanding the nature of QI and its role in human life.
