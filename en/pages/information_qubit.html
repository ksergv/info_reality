What Is an Information Qubit?

This article presents the hypothesis that the elementary structural unit of the informational field is the Information Qubit—the primary carrier of information whose collective interactions give rise to all observable physical phenomena.

Limitations of the Modern Physical Picture

Quantum mechanics and quantum field theory describe experimental observations with extraordinary accuracy. However, they primarily explain how physical processes occur, rather than why nature is organized in this particular way.

Several fundamental questions therefore remain open.

  • Why is nature described by a wave function?
  • Why do complex numbers play a fundamental role?
  • Why are probabilities determined by the Born rule?
  • Why do quantum states exhibit coherence?

In all these cases the mathematical formalism works remarkably well, yet mathematics itself does not explain the origin of the underlying regularities.

The Informational Field as Primary Reality

The proposed hypothesis starts from a different premise.

Primary reality is not the physical universe but a continuous informational field.

Matter, energy, space, time, and quantum fields are merely different manifestations of a deeper informational structure.

Just as an ocean consists of water molecules, the informational field consists of elementary informational units.

These units are referred to as Information Qubits.

Information Qubit

An Information Qubit is not the same as the quantum qubit of modern physics.

A quantum qubit is a mathematical description of the state of a physical quantum system.

An Information Qubit is regarded as a deeper entity that exists prior to the emergence of physical properties.

It is not a particle.

It is not a field in the physical sense.

It is an elementary carrier of informational state.

Each Information Qubit possesses its own state and is capable of coherent interaction with neighboring Information Qubits.

The totality of these interactions forms the structure of the informational field.

Informational Gradients

If Information Qubits possess different states, differences inevitably arise between regions of the informational field.

These differences form informational gradients.

Informational gradients determine the possible directions in which physical processes develop.

In other words,

> energy does not create information,

rather,

> energy follows informational gradients.

Physical processes are therefore secondary manifestations of the redistribution of information.

Quantum Mechanics as a Projection of the Informational Field

Within this model, quantum mechanics is not rejected.

On the contrary, it is regarded as an exceptionally accurate description of the physical projection of the informational field.

This leads to the following hierarchy.

Informational Field
        ↓
Information Qubits
        ↓
Informational Gradients
        ↓
Quantum States
        ↓
Physical Fields
        ↓
Matter

The physical qubit becomes the interface between the informational level and the observable physical world.

Why Complex Numbers Appear

One of the most remarkable features of quantum mechanics is its use of complex numbers.

Modern theory accepts them as part of the mathematical formalism.

However, the question remains why nature requires complex amplitudes at all.

Within the proposed model, the complex component of the wave function receives a different interpretation.

A complex number is not considered a fundamental entity.

Instead, it is viewed as a mathematical projection of those degrees of freedom of the Information Qubit that are inaccessible at the level of direct physical observation.

In other words,

> the complex amplitude is the mathematical trace of the Information Qubit.

The imaginary unit therefore becomes more than a convenient mathematical construct—it points toward the existence of a deeper level in the organization of reality.
