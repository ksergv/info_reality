What is QI?

Hypothesis about the Physical Nature of ЦИ

In this concept, ЦИ is the physical manifestation of coherent phase coupling between the informational level of reality and the quantum and physical levels of reality.

The informational level of reality is understood as fundamental informational structure interacting with the quantum and physical levels of reality.

The fundamental informational structure is regarded as the primary foundation underlying all coherent systems. It is not a separate physical field, but possesses a certain degree of coherence and contains informational states characterized by phase.

When coherence is established between an informational state and a quantum system, phase coupling arises between them, allowing the informational structure to manifest at the physical level.

\[ \Phi_I = A_I e^{i\phi_I} \]

where \(A_I\) is the amplitude of the informational state, and \(\phi_I\) is its phase.

A change in the informational state is accompanied by a change in phase:

\[ \phi_I \rightarrow \phi_I + \delta\phi_I \]

The fundamental informational structure can be regarded as quantized: its informational states form discrete quanta of an informational degree of freedom characterized by a specific phase. In this sense, an informational qubit can be regarded as the minimal coherent informational state capable of carrying and modifying informational phase.

When coherent coupling between the informational and quantum levels is established, a connection arises:

\[ \Phi_I \leftrightarrow \Psi_Q \]

A phase perturbation of the informational structure can then modify the phase of the quantum state:

\[ \phi_Q' = \phi_Q + \delta\phi_I \]

Here, \(\phi_Q\) is the initial phase of the quantum state, \(\delta\phi_I\) is the phase perturbation of the informational level, and \(\phi_Q'\) is the resulting phase of the quantum state.

Thus: In this model, ЦИ is neither a separate material substance nor an independent physical field. It is the physical manifestation of coherent phase coupling between the fundamental informational structure and the quantum state, through which the informational state can manifest itself in physical reality.

The process can be represented schematically as follows:

\[ \text{informational structure} \rightarrow \text{phase perturbation} \rightarrow \text{informational quantum} \] \[ \rightarrow \text{coherent coupling} \rightarrow \text{quantum field} \rightarrow \text{physical manifestation} \]

In this understanding, ЦИ is the carrier and manifestation of coherent phase coupling between the informational and physical levels of reality.

The reasoning presented here is a hypothesis and does not constitute an established position of modern physics. However, it may be useful for understanding the nature of ЦИ and its role in human life.
