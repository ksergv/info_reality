What Is Information Decoherence?

Modern physics explains the emergence of observable reality through the concept of quantum decoherence.

According to this model, when a quantum system interacts with its environment, it loses the ability to exist simultaneously in multiple potential states and transitions into an observable state.

Before a specific outcome appears, many possible alternatives exist.

After interaction with an observer, only one of them becomes realized.

Now let us consider another possibility.

Suppose that the process of physical decoherence is determined at an informational level before it occurs in the physical world.

If this is the case, quantum decoherence is not the beginning of the process but one of its later stages.

We shall refer to this initial process as Information Decoherence.

Thus, Information Decoherence is the process through which one specific possibility is realized from a multitude of potential informational alternatives.

Information, rather than a material object, becomes the primary result of this selection.

Physical reality then represents the next stage of the process.

Information Decoherence is the process of selecting and actualizing one of many potential meanings existing within a unified informational field. Physical decoherence is a subsequent stage of this process, leading to the emergence of observable material reality.

This is why two people may perceive the same event differently.

The physical situation remains the same.

However, differences in perception and thinking lead each observer to a different meaning.

People then establish a common language, concepts, and interpretations.

In this way, what we call Information Decoherence arises.

Within the proposed model, objectivity does not imply the existence of a single possible description of reality.

Instead, it represents a sufficiently stable agreement among individual consciousnesses.

Such agreement is necessary for collective life and activity...
