What Is the Information Field?

Modern physics successfully describes the behavior of matter and energy, but it provides virtually no answer to the question of what fundamental reality itself actually is. This article considers the hypothesis that the elementary structural unit of the information field may be regarded as an information qubit — a primary carrier of information whose collective interactions give rise to all observable physical phenomena.

Limitations of the Modern Physical Picture

Quantum mechanics and quantum field theory describe experimental results with extraordinary accuracy. However, they primarily answer the question of how processes occur, rather than why they are structured in precisely this way.

In particular, the following questions remain open:

  • why nature is described by a wave function;
  • why complex numbers play a fundamental role;
  • why probabilities are determined by the Born rule;
  • why quantum states possess coherence

In all these cases, the mathematical formalism works remarkably well, but mathematics itself does not explain the origin of the regularities it describes.

The Information Field as Primary Reality

The proposed hypothesis proceeds from a different premise.

The primary reality is not the physical Universe, but a continuous information field.

Matter, energy, space, time, and quantum fields are merely different manifestations of a deeper informational structure.

Just as an ocean consists of water molecules, the information field consists of elementary informational units.

These units may be called information qubits.

The Information Qubit

An information qubit is not a quantum qubit in the sense of modern physics.

A quantum qubit is a mathematical description of the state of a physical quantum system.

An information qubit is considered a deeper entity that exists before physical properties emerge.

It is not a particle.

It is not a field in the physical sense.

It is an elementary carrier of an informational state.

Each information qubit possesses its own state and is capable of coherently interacting with surrounding information qubits.

It is precisely the totality of these interactions that forms the structure of the information field.

Informational Gradients

If information qubits possess states, then differences inevitably arise between different regions of the field.

These differences form informational gradients.

Informational gradients determine the possible directions of development of physical processes.

In other words,

> energy does not create information,

but

> energy follows informational gradients.

Physical processes are therefore secondary manifestations of processes involving the redistribution of information.

Quantum Mechanics as a Projection of the Information Field

In this model, quantum mechanics is not rejected.

On the contrary, it is regarded as an exceptionally precise description of the physical projection of the information field.

This leads to the following hierarchy.

Information field
        ↓
Information qubits
        ↓
Informational gradients
        ↓
Quantum states
        ↓
Physical fields
        ↓
Matter

The physical qubit becomes an interface between the informational level and the observable physical world.

Why Do Complex Numbers Appear?

One of the most unusual features of quantum mechanics is the use of complex numbers.

Modern theory accepts them as part of its mathematical formalism.

However, the question remains open as to why nature requires the use of complex amplitudes at all.

In the proposed model, the complex component of the wave function receives a different interpretation.

A complex number is not a fundamental entity.

It represents a mathematical projection of those degrees of freedom of the information qubit that are absent at the level of direct physical observation.

In other words,

> a complex amplitude is the mathematical trace of an information qubit.

The imaginary unit then ceases to be merely a convenient mathematical object and becomes an indication of the existence of a deeper level of organization of reality.

The Born Rule

In modern quantum mechanics, probability is determined by the squared modulus of the wave function.

This rule works successfully in experiments, yet it is accepted as a fundamental postulate.

In the proposed model, the Born rule can be viewed differently.

The operation of calculating the squared modulus represents a transition from an informational state to a physically observable result.

In other words, physical probability arises as a projection of a hidden informational structure.

In this case, the Born rule is not a primary law of nature, but a consequence of the interaction between the physical interface and the informational level.

Consciousness as an Information Interface

If the information field is indeed the fundamental level of reality, the question arises as to whether it can be directly perceived.

Physical instruments register only physical fields.

Consequently, they can measure only manifestations of the information field, but not the field itself.

The proposed hypothesis allows for the existence of another means of interaction.

That means is consciousness.

However, the ordinary state of consciousness interacts predominantly with the physical interface.

Only when a high degree of internal coherence is achieved does consciousness become capable of directly perceiving informational structures.

In this model, coherent consciousness acts not as a source of information, but as a kind of resonant receiver capable of becoming synchronized with the coherence of the information field.
