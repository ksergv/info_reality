Coherence and binding energy.

If the information structure determines the system of relationships between its elements, then the degree to which these relationships are preserved can be regarded as a measure of the coherence of the structure.

The higher the level of coherence, the more elements of the system retain their mutual connectedness within a unified informational image. When transitioning to a lower level of organization, some of these relationships are lost or transformed into specific structural connections. Information becomes more definite, but at the same time loses part of its original integrity.

It is precisely at this transition that the concept of binding energy arises.

Binding Energy into Structure

The energy of a physical system does not exist independently of its organization. It is distributed among the elements of the system and can be bound by a specific configuration of their interactions.

Within the proposed model, this principle is considered much more broadly than the conventional concept of chemical, electromagnetic, or nuclear binding energy.

When a structure is formed, a specific set of interrelationships becomes fixed. Energy becomes bound to this structure and becomes part of its stable state.

The sequence can be represented as follows:

field → particles → bound particles → atoms → molecules → material structures.

Each successive level represents a more definite organization of the preceding one. At the same time, the original informational integrity gradually decreases, while energy becomes bound to increasingly specific structures.

Therefore, within the framework of this model, one can speak of a sequential transition:

high coherence → decoherence → structuring → energy binding.

In the reverse process, structural connections are broken down:

destruction of structure → release of binding energy → increase in the number of accessible states.

Thus, within the proposed model, binding energy is regarded as one of the manifestations of the process of structuring information at the physical level.

Coherence and Levels of Reality

If informational reality represents a higher level of organization, then its coherence should be substantially higher than the coherence of its physical realization.

In this model, the virtual body contains a holistic informational image of the organism. Within it, informational relationships between individual elements are preserved more completely than in the physical body.

The physical body is a realization of this image at a lower level. Therefore, in the transition from the virtual structure to the physical one, a certain loss of coherence is inevitable.

This sequence can be represented as follows:

Unified information field
        ↓
Informational virtuality
        ↓
Holistic informational image
        ↓
Informational decoherence
        ↓
Quantum fields
        ↓
Energy distributions
        ↓
Particles
        ↓
Atoms
        ↓
Molecules
        ↓
Physical body

At each successive level, the original informational structure is realized with a lower degree of integrity. At the same time, increasingly specific relationships emerge, in which the energy of the physical system becomes fixed.

Informational Relationships and Energy Distribution

In the previous sections, it was proposed that information qubits determine the system of informational relationships, while high-frequency yin–yang transformation provides the transition from informational structure to the probabilistic distribution of energy in the quantum field.

In this case, the energy of the quantum field is not distributed arbitrarily. Its probabilistic configuration is determined by the informational structure that the field realizes.

This leads to a fundamental proposition of the proposed model:

> Informational relationships determine the distribution of energy, while the distribution of energy realizes the informational structure at the physical level.

This proposition makes it possible to regard material structure as the result of a sequential transformation of information into a system of energetically bound states.

Decoherence of the Physical Body

The physical body is not an unchanging realization of its virtual image.

Over time, it interacts with the environment, changes, and loses and acquires relationships. Part of its original informational coherence is gradually disrupted.

Within the proposed model, this process can be regarded as a gradual informational decoherence of the physical body relative to its virtual image.

At the same time, the corresponding distribution of energy also changes.

Therefore, aging, damage, and impaired functioning of the organism can be regarded not only as a sequence of physical changes, but also as a gradual increase in the divergence between the virtual informational structure and its physical realization.

Restoration as a Reverse Process

If decoherence represents a transition from a more integrated structure to a less connected one, then restoration can be regarded as the reverse process.

A person does not necessarily have to create a new informational structure. It already exists at the level of the virtual body.

Within this model, the task of restoration is to restore the connection between the physical organism and this more coherent structure.

Of particular importance in this process is the middle dantian, associated with the region of the heart. It is regarded as a central area through which holistic perception of the virtual body may take place.

When this perception is restored, it is not individual images of organs that are restored, but the holistic system of interrelationships within the organism.

Schematically, the process can be represented as follows:

Virtual body
        ↓
Holistic perception
        ↓
Restoration of informational relationships
        ↓
Change in the probabilistic distribution of energy
        ↓
Redistribution of energy
        ↓
Restoration of physical structures

From this perspective, the sensation of an influx of energy when establishing a connection with the virtual body does not imply the appearance of new energy from an unknown source.

It may be a subjective manifestation of the redistribution of energy in accordance with the restored informational structure.

Energy that was previously bound within less coherent physical structures becomes available for their reorganization.

Coherence as the Direction of Restoration

Thus, in the proposed model, health can be regarded not only as the preservation of the physical integrity of the organism, but also as the preservation of correspondence between several levels of its organization.

The virtual body contains the original, more coherent informational structure.

The physical body is its constantly changing realization.

The greater the divergence between these levels, the higher the degree of informational decoherence.

Restoring the connection with the virtual body reduces this divergence and returns the physical system to a more coherent state.

In its most concise form, the principle can be expressed as follows:

> Coherence determines the system of informational relationships. Informational relationships determine the probabilistic distribution of energy. The distribution of energy forms the physical structure. Restoring informational relationships creates the conditions for restoring the physical structure.

Thus, informational coherence becomes not only a characteristic of an informational system, but also a possible fundamental factor determining the direction of its physical realization.

In this model, binding energy is a consequence of structuring, while the restoration of coherence represents the reverse movement — from a fragmented physical realization toward a more integrated informational organization.
