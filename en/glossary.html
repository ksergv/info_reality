Glossary

Virtual Body

The Virtual Body is a localized informational structure within the Unified Field of Consciousness through which consciousness interacts with the material world. In the model proposed in this book, the Virtual Body constitutes the true basis of human self-identification, while the physical body serves as its material interface.

Unified Information Field

The primary informational reality containing the potential states of all objects and processes. Individual consciousnesses are regarded as localized structures within this unified field.

Informational Decoherence

The process by which an informational structure transitions from a multiplicity of potential states to a single actualized state. In the model proposed in this book, informational decoherence precedes the emergence of material reality.

Informational Hierarchy

The multilevel organization of the Information Field, in which different levels are distinguished by the degree of generality of the information they contain and by their corresponding degree of coherence. Higher levels contain more general informational structures.

Informational Structure

A system of interconnected informational relations that determines the properties of an object prior to its material manifestation.

Informational Interface

The set of mechanisms through which consciousness receives information from the Information Field and interacts with the physical world. In this book, the coordinated functioning of the heart, brain, and consciousness is regarded as such an interface.

Informational Resonance

A state of coherence between the informational structure of consciousness and a particular level of the Information Field. Informational resonance makes informational influence possible.

Informational Frequency

In this book, informational frequency refers to a frequency modulated by information corresponding to the frequency range of a particular channel of the Tree of Sefirot.

Coherence

The degree of internal consistency within the informational state of consciousness. The higher the coherence, the more stable the informational resonance and the more effective the interaction with the Information Field.

Code

A stable sequence of symbols, phonemes, or other informational elements used to produce a controlling influence.

Collective Consciousness

An informational structure that emerges when multiple individual consciousnesses are united by a common purpose, system of values, or activity. In this book, collective consciousness is regarded as a distinct level of the Informational Hierarchy.

Mental Image

An integrated informational representation of an object perceived by consciousness as a unified image. A Mental Image is the primary instrument of Image-Based Encoding.

Image-Based Encoding

A method of informational influence in which an informational structure is modified through Mental Images and their comparison with a Reference Image.

Self-Identification

A person's conception of who or what they are. In the model proposed in this book, spiritual development is associated with a shift in self-identification from the Social Personality and the Physical Body to the Virtual Body.

Social Personality

The totality of social roles acquired by a person throughout life. The Social Personality is an external level of the manifestation of consciousness, but not its essential nature.

Control Code

A deliberately constructed vibrational sequence designed to create Informational Resonance and modify the Informational Structure of an object.

Level of Access

The highest level of the Informational Hierarchy at which consciousness is capable of sustainably maintaining Coherence and Informational Resonance.

Physical Body

The material interface of the Virtual Body through which consciousness interacts with physical reality.

Phoneme

The smallest stable unit of sound used in the formation of vibrational Control Codes.

Egregore

A collective informational structure that emerges through the sustained association of consciousnesses around a shared idea, activity, or system of values.

Reference Image

An informational representation of an object in its most harmonious state. It is used as a basis for Image-Based Encoding and informational therapy.

Informational Nature of Reality

The hypothesis that information constitutes the primary foundation of existence and that material reality emerges as a result of processes of Informational Decoherence.

Who Am I?

The central philosophical question of this book, running through all of its chapters. Within the model proposed here, the answer is that a human being is neither the Physical Body nor the Social Personality, but the Virtual Body—a localized structure of the Unified Information Field temporarily interacting with the material world through a physical organism.
