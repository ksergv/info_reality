Why Does Quantum Mechanics Use Complex Numbers?

Complex numbers play a fundamental role in quantum mechanics. They are used to describe wave functions, interference, and quantum evolution.

The mathematical formalism works with remarkable accuracy, yet the reason why nature requires complex numbers remains an open question.

In conventional quantum theory, complex amplitudes are accepted as part of the mathematical framework.

The informational model presented in this book proposes a different interpretation.

Complex amplitudes are viewed as mathematical projections of hidden informational degrees of freedom associated with Information Qubits.

From this perspective, the imaginary unit is not merely a mathematical convenience but a reflection of a deeper informational structure.

Complex numbers therefore become indicators of a level of reality that lies beneath observable physical phenomena.
