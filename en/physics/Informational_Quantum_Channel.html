Information-Quantum Channel

Abstract

Axiom: The informational level of reality is understood as a fundamental informational structure represented as the informational component of a complex quantum state and manifested through its phase structure.

This appendix considers a hypothetical mechanism of connection between the informational level and its physical realization. In the proposed model, the connection between the informational and quantum levels is bidirectional, although asymmetric in its function.

The informational structure defines the initial image, which is realized through the quantum level:

I→Q

After phase coupling has been established, the image can also be exchanged in the reverse direction, from physical reality to the informational level.

QYin↔QYang

The transfer of information requires a communication channel whose function is to transmit the informational structure-image to the quantum level.

C.1 Information-Quantum Channel

The fundamental informational structure is regarded as a primary foundation underlying coherent systems. It is not a separate physical field, but contains informational states characterized by a specific phase.

When coherence is achieved between the informational state and the quantum system, phase coupling arises, creating a channel of informational interaction.

ΦI ↔ ΨQ

In this model, phase is the condition for tuning the channel, while the image is the content of the exchange. Therefore, when phase coupling is present, the image can be transmitted both from the informational level to physical reality and from physical reality to the informational level.

C.2 Phase Coupling

Let the informational state be characterized by the phase φI , and the quantum state by the phase φQ . Then the condition for their coupling is phase correspondence:

Δφ = φI − φQ ≈ 0

With phase tuning, a stable coupling channel arises and the exchange of images becomes possible. When phase correspondence is disrupted, the channel weakens or ceases.

Thus, phase is not the content of informational exchange, but the condition that makes it possible.

C.3 The Image as the Content of Informational Exchange

Phase coupling itself is not the information being transmitted. It creates the condition under which an image can be transmitted through the channel. The image is regarded as a structured form of information capable of manifesting at the quantum and physical levels.

The informational field does not predetermine the direction of this change; the possibility of establishing a new form is determined by the process of its physical realization itself and by the stability of the coupling.

The initial image of the informational level receives a physical realization:

OI → OR

Physical reality, in turn, can return the image formed during the process of physical realization:

OR → OI

Thus, the exchange of images is bidirectional. At the same time, the informational level is not an immutable reference: stable changes arising in physical reality may become part of the updated informational structure.

C.4 CI as a Physical Manifestation of Coupling

In the proposed model, CI is not regarded as a separate material substance or an independent physical field. CI is the physical manifestation of coherent phase coupling between the fundamental informational structure and the quantum state.

Schematically, the process can be represented as follows:

informational structure → phase coupling → quantum state → CI → physical manifestation

In the reverse direction, physical reality can also transmit the image through the same channel while maintaining phase coupling.

In this understanding, CI is the carrier and manifestation of the coherent phase connection between the informational and physical levels of reality.

C.5 Complex Amplitude and the Born Rule

The quantum state is described by a complex amplitude:

Ψ=a+bi

Here, the real and imaginary parts are mathematical components of the complex quantum state. Within the proposed hypothesis, the complex structure is regarded as a possible mathematical interface between the informational structure and its quantum realization.

The imaginary unit i is not itself a physical field. Such an identification is an interpretation of the proposed model.

For the complex-conjugate state, we have:

Ψ*=a−bi

The complex-conjugate state is not interpreted here as the reverse direction of the informational channel. Complex conjugation is a mathematical operation necessary for calculating the squared modulus.

|Ψ|2=Ψ*Ψ=a2+b2

In standard quantum mechanics, the squared modulus of the wave function is related to the probability of detecting the state according to the Born rule.

In the proposed model, this mathematical operation may be interpreted as a transition from the complex description of the quantum state to a physically observable quantity.

C.6 Coherence and Memory of the Informational Interface

The quality of informational–quantum coupling in a human being is characterized by a measure of coherence C. In this context, the human informational interface is understood as the set of processes through which the informational structure is coupled to the quantum level of the physical organism.

At the quantum level, the proposed model assumes a high-frequency Yin–Yang interaction. The continuity and stability of this process provide the conditions for maintaining phase coupling and, consequently, for maintaining stable informational exchange.

At complete coherence:

C = 1

As coherence decreases, the degree of informational decoherence increases:

D = 1 − C

In the proposed model, coherence is not a static quantity. It is related to the history of informational exchange. Each stable act of coupling contributes to the memory of the system and may thereby increase the stability of subsequent cycles of exchange.

The memory of the system is regarded as a property of the coupling process itself. It does not belong exclusively to either the informational or the physical level, but is formed through their stable interaction.

The accumulated coherent memory can be hypothetically represented as an integral over time:

M (t) = ∫ 0 t a (τ) dτ

where a(τ) is the current coherence of an elementary act of exchange, and M(t) is the accumulated coherent memory of the system.

In this sense, memory provides continuity to the process: the previous history of stable coupling influences the ability of the system to maintain coherence and continue informational exchange. When coherence is maintained over a long period, the memory of the system increases; when coupling is repeatedly disrupted, its stability decreases.

Thus, informational decoherence does not mean destruction of the fundamental informational structure. It means a reduction in the stability of the coupling and in the system's ability to preserve the history of coherent exchange.

C.7 Stability and Evolution of the Image

Physical reality is not static. During physical realization, changes and new images may arise. When phase coupling is present, these changes may participate in reverse informational exchange.

If the image in physical reality coincides with the informational image:

OR = OI ⇒ coupling is maintained

no additional change to the informational structure occurs. The system continues to exist in a coherent state.

If an image arises in physical reality that does not correspond to the stable informational structure, it does not necessarily have to be eliminated immediately. It has the possibility to manifest and exist in physical reality. However, when the required phase coupling is lost, such an image ceases to participate in informational exchange.

Thus, the informational field does not determine in advance which changes may arise in physical reality. The possibility of generating a new image remains open to all variants.

If the resulting form proves to be nonviable or unstable, its existence comes to an end, and the corresponding image is not incorporated into the informational structure.

If, however, a new form proves stable and persists in physical reality, it may continue to participate in informational exchange. Through the reverse channel, the new image may become part of an updated informational structure:

OR → OI ′

This produces a sequence of evolutionary states:

OI → OR → OI′ → OR′ → ⋯

Such a process may be considered a hypothetical mechanism for the accumulation of stable changes within the informational structure. Adaptation in this case is not a purposeful action of the informational field. It emerges from the interaction of physical variability, stability of forms, and the ability to maintain phase coupling.

In this sense, the informational field both preserves the established image and remains capable of changing it. The evolution of the informational structure becomes a consequence of continuous exchange between the informational and physical levels.

C.8 Vibrational Codes

In the proposed model, vibrational codes are not regarded as an independent source of energy transfer to the informational level. They may act as low-frequency phase modulation of the processes that provide informational-quantum coupling.

Such modulation changes the degree of phase alignment:

Δφ=φI−φQ

As phase mismatch decreases, structural correspondence increases and, according to the hypothesis, so does channel coherence.

As phase mismatch increases, informational decoherence arises.

Thus, in the proposed model, the primary factor is the phase organization of informational-quantum coupling, whereas energy is regarded as a characteristic of an already physically realized quantum process.

C.9 Possibility of Experimental Verification

If the proposed model corresponds to reality, what should be measured directly is not the informational level itself, but the physical manifestations of changes in the quality of informational-quantum coupling.

Experimental investigation in this case should be aimed at identifying reproducible physical processes sensitive to changes in phase alignment and the coherence measure C.

This makes it possible to move the concept of informational coherence from a purely philosophical category into the domain of testable physical consequences of the proposed hypothesis.
